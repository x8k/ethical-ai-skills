# Chapter VI: The Peculiar Combination of Functions

## Core Idea
Women's unique economic position combines domestic, reproductive, and social functions in ways that are unparalleled in other species. This combination creates a specialized economic role that both depends on and serves male economic activity, reinforcing the sexuo-economic relation.

## Frameworks Introduced
- **Functional Specialization by Sex**: The assignment of different economic and social functions to males and females.
- **Economic Interdependence**: How male and female roles reinforce each other within the sexuo-economic system.

## Key Concepts
- **Domestic Economy**: The sphere of economic activity traditionally assigned to women.
- **Reproductive Labor**: The unpaid work of bearing and raising children, economically valued differently from male labor.
- **Social Reproduction**: The maintenance of social structures through women's unpaid labor.

## Mental Models
- **Invisible economy**: Much of women's economic contribution is uncounted and unvalued in traditional economic measures.
- **Reinforcement loop**: Male economic dominance is maintained by female domestic specialization, which depends on male economic provision.

## Anti-patterns
- **Undervaluing domestic labor**: Treating reproductive and domestic work as economically insignificant.

## Key Takeaways
1. Women perform a unique combination of economic, reproductive, and social functions.
2. These functions are specialized by sex but economically interdependent.
3. The specialization reinforces the broader sexuo-economic system.
4. Traditional economics fails to account for women's unpaid contributions.
