# Chapter XI: Social Division

## Core Idea
The sexuo-economic relation creates artificial divisions within society that hinder social progress. This chapter examines how the separation of men's and women's economic roles creates inefficiencies, inequalities, and social tensions that could be resolved through integration.

## Frameworks Introduced
- **Division of Labor by Sex**: The assignment of different economic roles based on sex.
- **Inefficiency of Division**: How sex-based economic separation creates waste and reduces productivity.

## Key Concepts
- **Artificial Division**: Economic roles divided by sex rather than by aptitude or interest.
- **Social Waste**: The loss of potential when half the population is restricted in economic participation.
- **Integration Benefits**: The social and economic advantages of eliminating sex-based divisions.

## Mental Models
- **Division as inefficiency**: Sex-based economic separation reduces overall social productivity.
- **Whole society benefit**: Eliminating artificial divisions benefits everyone, not just women.

## Anti-patterns
- **Naturalizing division**: Assuming sex-based economic roles are natural or optimal.

## Key Takeaways
1. Sex-based division of economic labor is artificial, not natural.
2. This division creates social and economic inefficiencies.
3. Eliminating the division would increase overall productivity and well-being.
4. Both men and women are harmed by artificial economic separation.
