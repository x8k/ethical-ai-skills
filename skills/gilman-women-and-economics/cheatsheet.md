# Cheatsheet — Women and Economics

*Quick reference for Charlotte Perkins Gilman's core frameworks and insights*

---

## 📊 Core Frameworks at a Glance

| Framework | Key Insight | When to Use | Example Application |
|-----------|-------------|--------------|---------------------|
| **Economic Determinism** | Economic conditions are the primary modifiers of human development | Analyzing social behavior | Why do gender roles persist? → Economic dependence |
| **Sexuo-Economic Relation** | Unique human condition: sex-relation + economic dependence | Understanding gender inequalities | Prostitution as economic symptom, not moral failing |
| **Excessive Sex-Distinction** | Humans have unprecedented secondary sex differences | Identifying harmful gender norms | Challenging "natural" gender roles |
| **Social Pathology** | Social "diseases" have economic causes | Diagnosing social problems | Marital conflict → economic dependence |
| **Social Reconstruction** | Social positions are human creations, can be redesigned | Designing equitable systems | Eliminating sex-based job divisions |

## 🔍 Decision Trees

### Is this social problem solvable?
```
Social Problem?
├── Has economic causes? → YES → Solvable through structural change
│   └── Identify economic conditions → Address root causes
└── NO → Re-evaluate: likely has hidden economic dimensions
```

### Analyzing Gender Inequality
```
Gender Inequality?
├── Is it economic in nature? → YES → Apply sexuo-economic analysis
│   ├── Is it structural? → YES → Requires systemic change
│   └── Is it individual? → NO (all gender inequality is structural)
└── Is it cultural? → YES → But check for underlying economic causes
```

### Designing Social Reform
```
Reform Needed?
├── Address economic structures? → YES → Prioritize
├── Change cultural conceptions? → YES → Do first (conceptual precedence)
└── Implement gradually? → NO → Comprehensive vision required
```

## ⚖️ Key Principles

### DO ✅
- **Trace social problems to economic causes**
- **Treat gender as economic category**
- **Value unpaid reproductive labor**
- **Challenge "natural" assumptions about social conditions**
- **Design integrated economic systems**
- **Recognize historical entrenchment as obstacle, not justification**

### DON'T ❌
- **Moralize economic structures**
- **Separate sex-relation from economic relation**
- **Accept social conditions as inevitable**
- **Address symptoms without changing structures**
- **Undervalue domestic and care work**
- **Assume individual solutions for structural problems**

## 📈 Evolution of Sex-Relation

```
Animal Kingdom → Natural sex-unions → Peaceful practice
         ↓
   Humans → Monogamous marriage identified as optimal
         ↓
   + Economic Dependence → Distorted sex-relation
         ↓
   = Social problems (prostitution, marital conflict)
         ↓
   Understanding causes → Deliberate change possible
```

## 💡 Quick Diagnostic Questions

1. **Who benefits economically from this arrangement?**
2. **How does this condition affect economic independence?**
3. **What would change if economic dependence were eliminated?**
4. **Is this condition natural or historically entrenched?**
5. **What unpaid labor is being performed here?**

---

## 🎯 Solution Design Checklist

- [ ] Addresses economic structures, not just behaviors
- [ ] Recognizes historical and cultural dimensions
- [ ] Values previously unpaid labor
- [ ] Enables economic independence
- [ ] Challenges artificial divisions
- [ ] Includes both men and women in benefits
