# Glossary - Property Is Theft!

**Anarchism** — Political philosophy advocating stateless, classless society based on voluntary association and mutual aid; Proudhon is considered one of its founders (All works)

**Authority** — The power to command and be obeyed; Proudhon argues all authority represents the imposition of one will on another (Federative works)

**Bank of Exchange** — Proudhon's proposed mutual bank that would provide credit without interest based on mutual guarantee (Credit works)

**Capital** — Accumulated wealth used for production; under capitalism, a means of exploiting labor (Contradictions)

**Centralization** — Concentration of political or economic power at higher levels, removing it from local control (Federative works)

**Co-operation** — Voluntary joint action for mutual benefit; alternative to capitalist competition (Mutualism)

**Competition** — Market mechanism where producers compete; Proudhon identifies it as both necessary and destructive (Contradictions Ch05)

**Contradiction** — Opposing principles or forces within social institutions; Proudhon's dialectical method identifies and resolves contradictions (All works)

**Credit** — The provision of resources now in exchange for future repayment; Proudhon proposes mutual credit without interest (Contradictions Ch10)

**Delegation** — Election of representatives for specific purposes with recallable mandates; alternative to permanent authority (Federative Principle)

**Division of Labour** — Specialization of workers in specific tasks; increases productivity but creates dependency and alienation (Contradictions Ch03)

**Exchange** — The mutual transfer of goods or services; Proudhon argues it should be based on equivalent value (Contradictions Ch02, Justice)

**Exploitation** — The extraction of surplus value from labor by capital; the essence of capitalist property relations (All works)

**Federation** — Voluntary association of autonomous groups for shared purposes; Proudhon's proposed political organization (Federative Principle)

**Freedom** — The absence of coercion; for Proudhon, true freedom requires both negative liberty (from interference) and positive liberty (capacity for self-determination) (All works)

**Government** — The institution of authority; Proudhon famously declares "To be governed is to be..." followed by a long list of degradations (General Idea of Revolution)

**Hierarchy** — Organization by rank or authority; Proudhon opposes all hierarchical structures in favor of federal organization (Federative works)

**Interest** — Payment for the use of capital; Proudhon argues it represents exploitation and should be eliminated (Credit works)

**Justice** — The principle of giving each their due; for Proudhon, justice is synthetic, resolving contradictions rather than compromising between them (Justice in the Revolution)

**Labor / Labour** — Human productive activity; Proudhon distinguishes between labor as the source of value and property as its appropriation (Property Ch03)

**Liberty** — For Proudhon, liberty is not the right to do as one pleases, but the power to act according to one's own will within the limits of equal liberty for all (All works)

**Monopoly** — Exclusive control of a market or resource; the dialectical opposite of competition (Contradictions Ch06)

**Mutualism** — Proudhon's economic philosophy; system based on mutual exchange, co-operative production, and federal organization (All works)

**Mutual Credit** — Credit extended without interest based on reciprocal trust and mutual guarantee; key to Proudhon's economic proposals (Credit works)

**Natural Right** — Rights claimed to exist independently of human convention; Proudhon demonstrates property cannot be justified as a natural right (Property Ch02)

**Occupation** — Taking possession of unowned land; Proudhon argues occupation cannot create legitimate property rights (Property Ch02)

**Police** — State function of maintaining order; Proudhon analyzes police power as both necessary and oppressive (Contradictions Ch07)

**Possession** — Individual use and control of resources; distinguished from property as exclusive appropriation (Property Ch04)

**Prescription** — Long-term possession creating legal title; Proudhon argues it cannot create moral right (Property Ch03)

**Property** — Exclusive right to use and dispose of resources; Proudhon's famous formulation: "Property is theft!" (All works)

**Property is Theft!** — Proudhon's central thesis: property represents the appropriation of collective social value by individuals (All works)

**Reform** — Gradual transformation of institutions; Proudhon often prefers reform through building alternatives over violent revolution (General Idea of Revolution)

**Representative** — Person elected to serve a specific function with a recallable mandate; alternative to permanent political authority (Federative works)

**Revolution** — Sudden transformation of society; Proudhon argues revolution is necessary but must include institution-building (General Idea of Revolution)

**State** — The centralized political institution; Proudhon analyzes the state as perpetuating exploitation through taxation and police power (Contradictions Ch07)

**Subsidiarity** — Principle that decisions should be made at the most local level possible (Federative Principle)

**Taxation** — State extraction of wealth; Proudhon argues "Taxation is theft" parallel to property (Contradictions Ch07)

**Universal Consent** — Claim that society collectively agrees to property; Proudhon demonstrates this does not exist (Property Ch03)

**Usury** — Charging interest on loans; Proudhon opposes all forms of usury as exploitation (Credit works)

**Value** — Economic worth; Proudhon analyzes the contradiction between use-value and exchange-value (Contradictions Ch02)

**Working Class** — Those who must sell their labor to survive; Proudhon argues they have the capacity for self-organization (Political Capacity of Working Classes)
