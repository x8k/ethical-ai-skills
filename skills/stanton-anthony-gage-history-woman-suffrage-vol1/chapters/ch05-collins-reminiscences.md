# Chapter 5: Mrs. Collins' Reminiscences

## Core Idea
Personal reminiscences of early women's rights activist Emily Collins, detailing the formation of the first suffrage societies, experiences with domestic violence, and the struggle for women's education and economic rights.

## Key Concepts
- First Suffrage Society
- Methodist class-leader and domestic violence
- Theology as a constraint on women
- Women and slaves: parallel status
- First medical college opened to women (Geneva, N.Y.)
- Petitions to the Legislature
- Property rights for women
- Dr. Elizabeth Blackwell
- Ernestine L. Rose

## Mental Models
- Use personal narratives to understand systemic oppression
- Recognize intersections between different forms of oppression
