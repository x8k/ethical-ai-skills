# Chapter 6: Ohio

## Core Idea
Ohio as the "promised land of fugitives" and a center of early women's rights organizing, with multiple conventions and legislative efforts.

## Key Concepts
- Ohio as refuge for escaped slaves and reformers
- Uncle Tom's Cabin influence
- Salem Convention (1850)
- Akron Convention (1851)
- Massilon Convention (1853)
- Address to the Women of Ohio
- Mohammedan law analogy (exclusion of women)
- New York Tribune coverage
- Cleveland Convention (1853)
- Joshua R. Giddings
- Horace Greeley
- Mary Wollstonecraft influence
- William Henry Channing's Declaration
- Pulpit and public sentiment
- Antoinette L. Brown
