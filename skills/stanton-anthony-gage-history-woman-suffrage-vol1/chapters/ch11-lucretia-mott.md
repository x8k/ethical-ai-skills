# Chapter 11: Lucretia Mott

## Core Idea
Biography and eulogy of Lucretia Mott, a central figure in the women's rights and abolition movements.

## Key Concepts
- Quaker background
- Anti-slavery activism
- Women's rights leadership
- Seneca Falls Convention co-organizer
- Memorial Services (1881)
- Elizabeth Cady Stanton's eulogy
- Quaker influence on women's rights
- Intersection of abolition and suffrage
