# Cheatsheet — The Enfranchisement of Women

*Quick reference for arguments and counter-arguments*

## 🎯 Core Arguments at a Glance

| Argument | Type | Key Principle | Counter to |
|----------|------|---------------|-----------|
| **Democratic Consistency** | Justice | All men are created equal → includes women | Tradition, separate spheres |
| **Taxation & Representation** | Justice | No taxation without representation | Exclusion from politics |
| **Talent Utilization** | Expediency | Society benefits from all talents | Wasted potential |
| **Social Harmony** | Expediency | Equality reduces conflict | Social instability |
| **Historical Parallel** | Precedent | Like slavery, women's subjection will end | It's always been this way |

## 🔥 Quick Counter-Arguments

### To: "Women belong in the home"
**Response**: "The proper sphere for all human beings is the largest and highest which they are able to attain to. What this is cannot be ascertained without complete liberty of choice."

### To: "Women are too emotional for politics"
**Response**: Historical evidence shows women have governed effectively (Elizabeth I, Isabella of Castile). The claim reflects stereotypes, not reality.

### To: "It's always been this way"
**Response**: Slavery was once considered natural. Religious intolerance was once accepted. Democratic revolutions were once radical. All were overcome.

### To: "Women's participation will depress wages"
**Response**: Even if true, women become partners rather than dependents. Society gains from women's economic contributions. Wage concerns can be addressed through other policies.

### To: "Public life will harden women's characters"
**Response**: The "hardening" influences of modern life affect women as much as men. Women already exhibit the full range of human traits. Character is shaped by circumstances, not sex.

## 📋 Argument Structure Templates

### For Women's Suffrage
```
Premise 1: All adults subject to laws have a right to a voice in making them
Premise 2: Women are adults subject to laws
Conclusion: Women have a right to suffrage

Supporting evidence:
- Democratic principles (Declaration of Independence)
- Taxation without representation is tyranny
- Women are tried by male juries without female peers
```

### For Equal Education
```
Premise 1: Education develops human faculties
Premise 2: Women have human faculties
Premise 3: Restriction prevents development
Conclusion: Women should have equal access to education

Supporting evidence:
- Women have succeeded in education when allowed
- Education enables economic independence
- Educated women are better mothers and citizens
```

### For Economic Partnership
```
Premise 1: Equal contribution deserves equal reward
Premise 2: Women contribute to industry (directly and indirectly)
Premise 3: Current system denies women equal reward
Conclusion: Women should be equal partners in economic life

Supporting evidence:
- Women's unpaid domestic labor enables men's paid labor
- Economic independence improves women's status
- Society benefits from women's full economic participation
```

## ⚖️ Decision Tree: Is This Objection Valid?

```
Objection Raised?
├── Does it apply to ALL women?
│   ├── NO → Individual circumstance, not universal justification
│   │   └── Rebut: Laws should not exclude all for individual cases
│   └── YES → Does it apply equally to men?
│       ├── NO → Sex-based discrimination, unjust
│       │   └── Rebut: Unequal treatment without justification
│       └── YES → Is it based on evidence or stereotype?
│           ├── Stereotype → Rebut with counter-examples
│           └── Evidence → Acknowledge, show benefits outweigh
└── Is it a principle or preference?
    ├── Preference → Not a valid justification for law
    └── Principle → Examine for consistency with democratic values
```

## 💡 Key Principles to Invoke

### DO ✅
- **Apply democratic principles consistently**
- **Use historical parallels** (slavery, religious freedom)
- **Point to women's historical achievements**
- **Show benefits to all of society**
- **Expose power interests behind "natural" claims**
- **Distinguish between individuals and groups**

### DON'T ❌
- **Accept "tradition" as justification**
- **Allow objections that apply to some to justify excluding all**
- **Cede the moral high ground**
- **Accept gradualism as an end, not a means**
- **Ignore the benefits to men of women's equality**

## 📚 Essential Quotations

> "All men are created equal" — This includes women, or the principle is meaningless.

> "Taxation and representation should be co-extensive." — Women pay taxes; women deserve representation.

> "The proper sphere for all human beings is the largest and highest which they are able to attain to."

> "Power makes itself the centre of moral obligation." — The powerful define virtues for the powerless.

> "The subjection of women... is an unqualified mischief; a source of perversion and demoralization."

> "It is tyranny to shut out one half of the competitors." — On the competition objection.
